By using OneStorey you agree to this. It is short because the product is simple: it records things, and it does not touch money.

You must be 18

OneStorey is for people aged 18 and over. You confirmed this when you made your account.

This is not a formality. Renting a room is a contract, and a person under 18 cannot enter one — so an app whose whole subject is tenancy has no business being used by somebody who cannot hold a tenancy. It also carries private messages between two people, photographs, and a search that shows people within five kilometres of each other, none of which is built for a child.

If we find an account belongs to somebody under 18, we close it and remove what it posted. If you believe a child is using OneStorey, write to thepanatagcompany@gmail.com and we will act on it.

What OneStorey is

A place-centred social network with record-keeping attached. An owner runs a group for their building; the people renting there join with a code; the app keeps the feed, the concerns and the rent record of what happened.

It is a record, not an authority. Nothing here is a legal document.

OneStorey never holds your money

Rent moves between a renter and an owner exactly as it did before — cash, bank transfer, GCash, whatever you already use. OneStorey records that both of you agreed it happened.

It is not an escrow, not a payment service, and not a guarantor. A dispute about money is between the two of you.

What is yours

What you write stays yours. By posting it you allow OneStorey to store it and show it to the people it was meant for — the members of that place — which is the only way a group can work.

You are responsible for what you post, and for having the right to post it.

What is not allowed

Posting somebody else’s private information. Harassment. Threats. Pretending to be somebody else. Using a place’s group to advertise to the people in it.

Trying to reach data that is not yours, by any route.

An owner may remove somebody from their place, and OneStorey may close an account that does these things.

What is not promised

The app is provided as it is. It is not promised to be available without interruption: it depends on a hosting provider and on your own connection.

OneStorey does not verify that a place is lawfully operated, that a listing is accurate, or that a person is who they say they are.

It does not take sides in a disagreement between a renter and an owner, and will not arbitrate one.

A tenancy record shows what was entered and confirmed inside the app. It is evidence of what the two of you recorded. It is not a receipt issued by an authority and it is not a contract.

Ending it

You can close your account at any time, in Profile. See the privacy notice for exactly what that removes and what it leaves.

A paid subscription, when one exists, runs to the end of the period already paid for.
